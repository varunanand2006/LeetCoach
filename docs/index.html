Progressive Hints

We won't just give you the answer. LeetCoach uses progressive hints to help you figure it out on your own. It starts by pointing you in the right direction, then reveals the algorithmic pattern, and finally the data structure. It's built to train your problem-solving skills the way a senior engineer would guide you.
code_blocks

Code Analysis

LeetCoach reviews your code to catch bugs and edge cases while calculating your Big-O time and space complexity. It compares your solution against the optimal approach, so you can learn how to write production-ready code instead of just passing the basic test cases.
schema

DSA Guidance & Chat

If you're stuck on a tricky algorithm, LeetCoach can act as your technical interviewer. It identifies the optimal patterns and asks you questions to challenge your logic. You'll have to defend your engineering trade-offs, giving you practice for real FAANG interviews.
account_tree

Visual Diagrams

See your algorithms come to life. LeetCoach can generate diagrams alongside any response — tree structures, state machines, flowcharts, and more — rendered right in the sidebar so you can visualize the approach instead of just reading about it.
summarize

Session Review Reports

When you're done with a problem, generate a full retrospective report. LeetCoach reviews your entire session — how you approached the problem, where you struggled, the optimal solution with its complexity, and what patterns to practice next. It's the debrief a mentor would give you.

Tailored to your goals

Switch between five distinct coaching modes depending on whether you're learning a new topic, grinding problems, or preparing for a real interview.

  • school

    Learn Mode

    Highly educational. Get detailed explanations of algorithms and line-by-line breakdowns.

  • fitness_center

    Practice Mode

    Minimal nudges. Receive just enough guidance to get unstuck without giving away the answer.

  • work

    Interview Mode

    Strict and Socratic. Simulates a real technical interview with probing questions and edge-case focus.

  • speed

    Optimize

    Get a focused efficiency review of your code. The current Big-O, runtime, and memory, as well as how to improve it.

  • rate_review

    Interview Feedback

    Get the debrief a real interviewer would give you. Honest, specific feedback on your approach, code quality, and complexity, exactly what you need to hear before your next interview.
